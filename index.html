OUR STORY

Celebrating Nigerian Heritage Through Fashion

Founded in Lagos in 2009, Heritage Apparel was born from a passion for preserving and modernizing Nigeria's rich textile traditions. Our founder, Adaeze Okonkwo, envisioned a brand where ancient weaving techniques meet contemporary design.

Every piece in our collection is meticulously crafted by skilled artisans using premium Nigerian fabrics — from hand-dyed Adire to intricately woven Aso-Oke. We believe fashion should honor the past while embracing the future.
